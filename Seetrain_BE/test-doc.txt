# Cybersecurity Basics: How to Stay Safe Online

## 1. Introduction

Cybersecurity protects computers, devices, networks,
and personal information from digital attacks.

People use the internet for communication, shopping,
banking, and work.

## 2. Common Online Threats

- Phishing uses fake messages to steal personal information.
- Malware is harmful software that can damage devices.
- Weak passwords make accounts easier to attack.
- Fake websites imitate trusted services.

## 3. Strong Passwords

- Use at least 12 characters.
- Combine uppercase and lowercase letters.
- Include numbers and special symbols.
- Avoid reusing passwords.
- Use a trusted password manager.

## 4. Safe Internet Browsing

- Check website addresses.
- Avoid suspicious links.
- Keep software updated.
- Download applications from trusted sources.
- Avoid sensitive activities on public Wi-Fi.

## 5. Protect Your Accounts

- Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
- Install security updates.
- Use antivirus software.
- Lock your devices.
- Back up important files.

## 6. Conclusion

Stay alert, use strong passwords,
and protect your personal information online.
